# Six ready-made Keepers

Everyone starts with Energy 6 and Embers 3. Every talent costs one Ember. An active talent can be used once per own turn; reactions are limited to one per hero per round. Alternative effects require separate uses. Follow the [rules](rules.md) on range, help and trouble.

| Keeper | Courage | Cunning | Wonder | Bond |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Nilo, bridge guardian | +3 | +1 | +0 | +2 |
| Mira, explorer of moving stairs | +1 | +3 | +2 | +0 |
| Luma, constellation reader | +0 | +1 | +3 | +2 |
| Tito, travelling repairer | +2 | +3 | +1 | +0 |
| Ada, collector of stories | +1 | +0 | +2 | +3 |
| Brina, fox of lost paths | +2 | +1 | +0 | +3 |

## Nilo

**Shelter — reaction:** when you or an ally in your zone would lose Energy from an attack or hazard, reduce that loss by 2, minimum zero. Decide after defense and before applying the loss. No lingering bonus.

Objects: oak shield, lantern. Ordinary attack: shield, Courage, own zone.

Wish: keep a road open for everyone. Fear: arriving too late. Promise: never abandon someone left behind. Keepsake: an unused ticket. Suggested bond: Mira showed him a path he could not see.

## Mira

**Impossible step — during movement:** cross up to two connections with your free move, ignoring one ordinary obstacle such as debris or a short gap. Cannot cross walls, sealed doors or unconnected zones. Grants no extra actions.

Objects: grappling hook and rope, sling. Ordinary attack: sling, Cunning, own or visible adjacent zone.

Wish: complete a changing map. Fear: no longer recognizing home. Promise: leave a trail for the next traveller. Keepsake: a compass pointing to the last promise kept; a narrative memory, not an automatic locator. Bond: Tito repaired her hook.

## Luma

**Gentle spell — action:** create a simple light, sound or motion of a small object in your zone or an adjacent one. To change an obstacle or influence a creature, check Wonder with advantage. Success can resolve one interaction or make an opponent Shaken, without Impact. Purely decorative effects cost no Ember or check but cannot bypass challenges. The talent does not remove explicit station requirements.

Objects: prism staff, chalk pouch. Ordinary attack: prism ray, Wonder, own or visible adjacent zone.

Wish: discover a new constellation. Fear: magic having only one correct use. Promise: ask before transforming someone else's belongings. Keepsake: a folded paper star. Bond: Ada knows a story about that star.

## Tito

**Improvised solution:** choose one use for one Ember: before your own repair/mechanical check, gain advantage; or as a reaction before an ally in your zone makes a check you can meaningfully assist, grant advantage without spending your action. This does not stack with another helper.

Objects: tool case, rope launcher. Ordinary attack: launcher, Cunning, own or visible adjacent zone; Impact represents entanglement.

Wish: build something that works without him. Fear: being useful only when things break. Promise: explain his inventions. Keepsake: a wooden screw. Bond: Nilo waited for him during a difficult journey.

## Ada

**Refrain — action:** choose another ally in your zone: restore 2 Energy, up to 6, or remove one condition. Works at zero but does not reset a used rescue. Describe a song, story or agreed gesture; actual singing is never required.

Objects: small drum, walking stick. Ordinary attack: stick, Courage, own zone. Bond instead serves appropriate dialogue and social objectives.

Wish: collect an unheard story. Fear: being remembered for someone else's words. Promise: ask permission before sharing another's story. Keepsake: a blank page sewn into her diary. Bond: Brina chose to tell her how she lost the trail.

## Brina

**Path instinct:** before a check about tracks, hidden passages or environmental hazards, gain advantage. You may use Bond instead of Cunning to interpret a living being's tracks by explaining which habit or relationship you recognize. Does not read minds or automatically find every secret.

Objects: trail hook, pocket cloak. Ordinary attack: hook, Courage, own zone. Small size does not grant invisibility or automatic passage.

Wish: find a home that changes with her. Fear: returning meaning giving up travel. Promise: respect others' paths. Keepsake: a leaf from an unseen tree. Bond: Luma promised to look for it.

Change names, appearance, pronouns and bonds freely before play. Keep the mechanical values and limits when comparing sessions of this version.
