# Workshop and atlas

The Library of Thresholds holds unfinished maps: they mark an entrance, never a mandatory ending. Keepers carry knowledge and memories between independent places. **Fading** describes the loss of possibilities when someone imposes a single story on others. Not every adventure needs this threat.

## Six original worlds

| World | Identity and proposed scene rule | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical Harbour | Terraces and bridges; shifting a counterweight changes an announced passage | The Fair of Tomorrows, already developed |
| Garden of Hours | Greenhouses follow different seasons; passages determine which growth stage is encountered | A seed always arrives too early |
| Bell Desert | Dunes marked by resonators; sounds and equivalent visual symbols identify routes | A bell points to an unmapped village |
| Tidal Archive | Documents in water-operated chambers; cycles change available rooms | Two truthful accounts seem incompatible |
| Comet Workshop | Travellers repair celestial bodies with temporary materials; every fix has an explicit duration | A comet wants a different orbit |
| City of Opaque Glass | Windows display agreed possibilities, never certain futures; display choices influence trust | Someone presents a possibility as a sentence |

Scene rules are prompts to specify per adventure, not balanced powers. None of these worlds requires another franchise's characters, plots or designs.

## Build an episode

Specify: place, one resident's wish, visible anomaly, opposing force, three connected zones, an essential clue available without a roll, secret, three solutions, failure consequence and final memory.

| d6 | Anomaly | Objective | Complication |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Directions change | Bring someone home | The guide owes a debt |
| 2 | An object remembers tomorrow | Rebuild a message | Available time is shrinking |
| 3 | A bridge selects its users | Renegotiate a rule | Two communities want different things |
| 4 | A room loses its exits | Protect a meeting | A shortcut consumes a resource |
| 5 | A project builds itself | Stop construction | Someone already lives there |
| 6 | A path splits | Recognize a promise | The opponent protects someone |

Roll once per column and adapt logically. Example: Comet Workshop + self-building project + stop construction + existing residents. A newly formed orbital bridge houses a small community: dismantling it fixes the technical problem but creates another. Prepare alternatives rather than one mandatory moral answer.

## Campaign formats

- Independent episodes: return to the Library after each session.
- Six stops: one episode per world, with a relationship changing on each return.
- Shared campaign: multiple groups record changes to one Library; appoint a continuity keeper.

Each chapter distinguishes player knowledge, facilitator knowledge and ways forward after failure. An accidentally shared secret document should not reveal the entire campaign.

## External content

Create original adventures, translations and adaptations under the licenses. Publishing material based on other works requires checking the relevant rights: renaming alone does not establish independence. Even an old tale can have protected modern translations, illustrations and adaptations. Contributions must be original or have verifiable provenance and licenses.
