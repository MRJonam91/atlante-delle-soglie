# New faces for the Keepers

Six original fairy-tale identities that can replace ready-made characters. Keep the referenced sheet's values and talent; change names, background, wishes and descriptive objects without gaining extra abilities. No external-story knowledge is required.

| Identity | Mechanical sheet | Wish | Personal difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Orimanto**, maker of opaque glass | Nilo | Build a shelter every resident can change | Confuses protection with deciding for others |
| **Corvinel**, comet courier | Mira | Deliver a letter to its future author | Fears opening a message about himself |
| **Mirilume**, prism reader | Luma | Find a colour for an unnamed possibility | Trusts first impressions too quickly |
| **Tessoria**, route stitcher | Tito | Sew a road people can repair together | Hides mistakes for fear of disappointing others |
| **Neravela**, tidal archivist | Ada | Return two conflicting testimonies to a village | Chooses quiet over a necessary conversation |
| **Velisca**, botanist of hours | Brina | Grow a garden with more than one season | Struggles to accept a plant's different needs |

## Appearance, voice and objects

- Orimanto carries frosted-glass plates, an apron and a wooden mallet. The mallet uses Nilo's same melee attack; it grants no resistances.
- Corvinel is a talking corvid with a travelling cloak and paper seals. In this version he crosses the same connections as everyone else; flight is descriptive and Mira's talent defines special movement.
- Mirilume wears a faceted visor and writes hypotheses on coloured ribbons. Her prism has Luma's same range and limits.
- Tessoria uses thread, clamps and a folding case. Her stitching follows Tito's repair/mechanical talent and creates no automatic portals.
- Neravela is an amphibious person with a waxed-paper cloak, clay tablets and a stick. This grants no automatic special breathing or access to other zones. Agree on unusual traits before play without bypassing challenges. Storytelling replaces Ada's refrain.
- Velisca is a small bark creature with grafting hands and a trail hook. Use Brina's rules; no immunity, instant growth or seasonal control is gained.

## Personal hooks

Orimanto must listen to future residents; Corvinel meets someone claiming to have received the letter already; Mirilume finds two observers seeing different things in the same prism; Tessoria must decide whether to admit a bad knot; Neravela seeks dialogue without erasing either testimony; Velisca needs someone who gardens differently.

For initial tests, each mechanical role remains occupied only once, regardless of name. Record the variant on the [session sheet](playtest.md). These names have not undergone professional trademark clearance.

## Create more names and people

Trades, materials and landscapes can evoke a fairy-tale genre: glass, routes, prisms, tides and seasons suggest a shared atmosphere. Design motivation, relationships, appearance and powers together; a different name alone does not establish independence from a recognizable existing character. Similarities sought here concern genre and atmosphere rather than character counterparts.
