# The Fair of Tomorrows

Original adventure for 3–5 Keepers, 0.1.0-playtest. Target duration 90–150 minutes, unverified. Contains facilitator secrets. No knowledge of other games or films is required.

## Ten-minute preparation

Read the [rules](rules.md), choose [Keepers](characters.md), show the [public board](../../print/board.svg). Each hero has Energy 6 and Embers 3; the group has Sparks 3. Prepare three Compass tokens, six timer boxes, one Echo token and Stability tokens equal to twice the number of heroes.

Use rounds only during conflict. No global timer before the finale. If the group splits, alternate short scenes so one player does not complete everything alone.

## Read aloud

“Your ticket has no date. It says: bring something you cannot do yet. Across the threshold, the docks of a vertical city host the Fair of Tomorrows. People exchange seeds, tools, promises and untried projects. But this year every sign points to the same destination.”

**Vertical Harbour** consists of terraces joined by counterweighted bridges. Its fair celebrates possibilities rather than selling guaranteed predictions. The Route Loom has begun merging every road into one compulsory path.

## People and the secret

- **Iria**, map printer, wants visitors to remain free to get lost. She knows where the three navigation tools were kept.
- **Oren**, bridge maintainer, wants to prevent accidents. He authorized a temporary repair and fears being blamed.
- **Tal**, travelling gardener, wants to reach a distant greenhouse. The compulsory road always brings him back.
- **Aster**, the copper construct maintaining the Loom, applied an incomplete instruction: “No one shall take a wrong turn.” It is not Oren in disguise or a servant of an external villain. It can learn to recognize consent and choice.

The secret is available, not locked behind a roll: Aster turned safety into control. Iria suspects this immediately; the log in C confirms it automatically. None of these people completes the finale for the heroes.

## Map and clues

Bidirectional connections: **A–B, B–C, B–D, C–E, D–E, E–F1, F1–F2, F1–F3**. F2 and F3 connect through F1. No necessary hidden passages. Players may inspect the board.

| Zone | Place | Available without a check |
|---|---|---|
| A | Arrival dock | The ticket opens entry |
| B | Project square | Iria points to the tools and E–F1 entrance |
| C | Route printshop | Incomplete instruction log and Voices Compass |
| D | Pulley bridge | Oren and Weight Compass |
| E | Departure garden | Tal, sentries and Step Compass |
| F1 | Counterweight desk | Weight station; finale entry |
| F2 | Map balcony | Voices station |
| F3 | Loom tower | Step station and Loom knot |

### A–B: choose a project

Each Keeper describes something they would like to learn at the fair. Iria asks them to recover three Compasses to correct the Loom; no passwords required. Record each carrier. Passing a Compass to an ally in the same zone is free, once per carrier's turn.

Optional Cunning 10 identifies how signs are rewritten. On failure the sign still shows the problem but the Keeper becomes Shaken. No other cost. Do not repeat this check to farm benefits.

### C: Voices Compass

The printing machine accepts one route at a time. Demonstrate two legitimate destinations by drawing or describing them to gain the Compass without a roll. Alternatively, Cunning or Wonder 13 changes the selector.

Failure still opens the selector but a mechanical paper sheet wraps around the Keeper's hands: Shaken. No lost clue. The log is readable regardless.

### D: Weight Compass

The bridge is rising with visitors and supplies still on it. Drop one useful ordinary resource, such as a rope or lantern, into a recovery net to free the counterweight: automatic success, object unavailable until the epilogue. A keepsake is never required.

Alternatively, Courage or Cunning 13 jams the pulley. On failure, Oren secures the passage and hands over the Compass; the Keeper loses 1 Energy. Oren solves no other objectives. Keeping a costly promise to protect visitors can award this scene's Spark, maximum one.

### E: Step Compass

Sentries confiscate luggage with incomplete itineraries, including Tal's portable nursery. Use **1 for three heroes, 2 for four, 3 for five**. They occupy E and never pursue beyond C or D.

**Spool sentry:** Guard 10, Stability 4. On activation, attack a hero already in its own zone: Cunning or Courage defense 10; failure loses 1 Energy. With no target, move one zone toward an intruder; it can occupy only C, D and E. At zero, deactivate. Shaken and Hindered work normally.

Alternatives: Bond 13 to demonstrate that Tal chose his route, or Cunning/Wonder 13 to correct the luggage reader. One success deactivates every sentry. The first failure begins conflict, but a new gesture or argument permits another attempt as an action on a hero's turn. Destruction is optional.

After resolution, Tal provides the third Compass. If everyone is in trouble, he moves them to safety: they still receive the Compass and each returns with 3 Energy; start the finale timer with one box marked.

The group may take its one safe break now if unused. Entering F1 starts the finale.

## Finale: correct one road without erasing the others

All heroes enter F1 before the first round. Missing Compasses do not seal the entrance: dialogue and direct action remain available; stations require their actual Compasses.

The Loom has **Guard 13 and Stability equal to twice the starting number of heroes**: 6, 8 or 10. Its knot is in F3 and can be targeted from F1 with ranged attacks. It is a structure and ignores Shaken/Hindered. Aster hears all F zones and is not a separate target.

Combine three approaches. One action cannot activate two approaches simultaneously.

1. **Recalibrate:** in the matching zone, the carrier spends an action with the corresponding Compass and Cunning or Wonder 13, or Bond 13 while coordinating a willing resident. F1 Weight, F2 Voices, F3 Step. Success permanently activates the station. **At most one new station per round**, as the Loom must settle. It also protects its zone during the current round's environment phase. Three active stations resolve the finale while preserving the Loom.
2. **Negotiate:** from any F zone, spend an action and check Bond or Wonder 13 using a concrete example of choice witnessed in the adventure. Three distinct contributions change the instruction; **at most one dialogue success per round**. Each success cancels only the current environment phase. Failure requires a new contribution before retrying; acting skill is never mandatory.
3. **Deactivate:** attack the knot against Guard 13. Each success inflicts 2 Impact; maneuvers cannot affect this structure. When a direct attack inflicts Impact, make **one Echo token** available. It never stacks; a new Echo replaces the previous one. Before the following round ends, spend it before a recalibration or dialogue check for advantage, then discard it. Stations inflict no Impact and generate no Echo. At zero Stability the knot stops: everyone is free, but fair bridges must be reopened manually in the epilogue.

Progress remains separate: two stations and one dialogue success do not count as three of the same objective. Protection and Echo still let plans help each other. Stop resolving actions and timers immediately when any objective is completed.

**Environment:** unless canceled through dialogue, select one hero in an unprotected F zone, rotating targets when possible. A Loom rope tries to return them to the assigned route: Cunning or Courage defense 13; failure loses 1 Energy. Only one target per round. If every occupied zone is protected, no attack. Protection expires at the end of this phase.

**Timer:** after the environment, mark one of six boxes. At six, Aster closes the fair for maintenance and sends everyone back to the dock. They stopped the compulsory route from continuing but have not reopened the roads; they must return with another project. If everyone is in trouble, apply this outcome immediately. Failed checks never mark additional boxes.

## Epilogue

Ask each hero which destination they add to the map. Recover objects left on the bridge. If the knot was deactivated, choose one bridge to reopen as a brief narrative action with no further rolls; the city helps with the rest.

Shared reward: **Unfinished map**, a memory usable once in a later adventure to gain one Spark, maximum six. Mark it spent afterward. It is not currency or an item available in unlimited copies.

Iria suggests another lead: paths in the **Garden of Hours** are bringing every seed to the same season. This is a hook for an adventure to create with the [workshop](workshop.md).

## Variants agreed before play

Families new to RPGs: keep the six-box timer, suggest alternatives and welcome drawings or gestures. Experienced groups: four-box finale timer and discuss what shared governance should replace Aster's instruction. Other statistics remain unchanged. Both variants require playtesting.
